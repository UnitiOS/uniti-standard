# 0022. Personal data marker

Date: 2026-10-17
Status: provisional (waiting for legal review, together with decision 0009)

**Context**
Redaction (decision 0009) may only touch data that concerns a person. It must
be decided how such data is recognized.

**Decision**
- Every property card states **yes** or **no**: whether its value is personal
  data.
- Only a fact with a property marked yes may be redacted.
- Changing the marker from no to yes may be done at the same address. From yes
  to no changes the meaning, so a new address is required.
- This whole decision is marked **[provisional]**.

**Alternatives**
- *The marker on the concept, not the property.* Rejected: within one concept,
  a person's name is personal data, but the date of their rental is not.
- *Graded levels of sensitivity.* Not yet decided; waiting for legal review.

**Consequences**
- Reviewed together with redaction before the two can become stable.
